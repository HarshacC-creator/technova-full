Questions clinic owners ask

Will patients know they’re talking to an AI?

Receptly introduces itself clearly, speaks naturally, and passes the call to your team when someone asks for a person.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You can forward your existing number to Receptly, so patients keep calling the same one.

What if it can’t answer something?

It takes a message or transfers the call, and the request appears in your dashboard so nothing is lost.

Which calendars does it work with?

Google Calendar to start. If you use another booking tool, ask us during the demo.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly with no long contract.
